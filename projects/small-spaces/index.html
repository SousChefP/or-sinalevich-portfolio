App Overview

About:

Drawing from IKEA’s values of practicality, flexibility, and empowerment, these illustrations were designed to depict everyday situations where people and objects bend and adjust to fit inside a small, contained space. Elements are bended in tight compositions & compressed into defined boundaries.

The color palette takes the famous IKEA blue & yellow, and adds a few more shades to create a fun, non-judgmental feel.
