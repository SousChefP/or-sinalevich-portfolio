Euka Tea

About:

Euka was developed as a modular product line, including individual tea bags, loose tea blends, and a personalized kit. Each format represents a different level of engagement with the product: from casual use to a more curated experience.

After researching the products available on the market, we understood the image psychoactive mushrooms could have, and built the design system around that. With grid-based layouts and monospace typography, referencing clinical and pharmaceutical systems, alongside a softer layer of handwritten labels and natural materials, we created a brand that communicates use in a clear but non-intimidating way.
