Overview:

An interactive website and visual identity for a children's podcast, designed to encourage active listening, engagement, and a way to join the conversation.

Made during "Design in Digital spaces" course, Bezalel, 2026, in collaboration with Mia Gaon.

My Role:

  • Visual Identity

  • Web Design

Let’s Talk
About It

Mini-Site Mobile Web Page

  1. Let's Talk About It is a children's podcast in which a professor and his 10-year-old daughter Naomi discuss complex subjects in a way kids can follow. After listening to some episodes, we understood that kids approach podcasts differently from adults, and wanted to understand how design could support their listening experience. Because Naomi is an active participant in the conversation, we wanted to create a platform where children at home could take part too while listening to the podcast.

  2. We decided to encourage participation through drawing. For each episode we illustrated a depiction of the subjects, allowing kids to color it while they listen on a tablet or with a mouse. The idea then expanded to the entire site’s visual identity, with open, imperfect illustrations that invite kids to add to them. We also made it our goal to keep the design accessible and pleasant without feeling childish.

  3. Drawing works as a layer across the entire site: a toolbar with colors, an eraser and a clear button on every page, and kids can draw anywhere on the desktop version and on episode players on a tablet. We designed the player illustrations to be more structured and closed, so kids begin with options to color instead of a blank page. We also created an Exhibition page in the site, where finished drawings can be saved and uploaded to.

    To keep the design from feeling childish, we kept a bold but neutral typography, and made the UI elements colorful to keep the young, fun feeling when the illustrations remained black lines. We designed everything in Figma, then built it using Framer.

  4. Our design for this mini-site aims to turn listening to a podcast about big subjects from a passive activity into an active one for children aged 6–12. Drawing while listening gives kids a way to respond to what they hear, and the exhibition lets them share it. Because the whole site can be drawn on in the desktop version, participation shapes the entire experience rather than a single podcast player feature. An accessible but mature design suits the podcast's subjects, and the unfinished, inviting illustrations make the children's additions fit in with harmony and become part of the finished product.
